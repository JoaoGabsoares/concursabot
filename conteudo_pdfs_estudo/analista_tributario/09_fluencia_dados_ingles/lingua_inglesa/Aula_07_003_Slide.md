IF-CLAUSES – FIRST CONDITIONAL

  IF + SIMPLE PRESENT, WILL /CAN/MAY/MUST


                    If I have money, I will travel.

                     I will call you If I have time.

                                    


---

Língua Inglesa – Prof . Adolfo Sá


IF-CLAUSES – FIRST CONDITIONAL

            IF + SIMPLE PRESENT, IMPERATIVE


  If you want to come with us, put on your sweater.

                                    


---

Língua Inglesa – Prof . Adolfo Sá


IF-CLAUSES – SECOND CONDITIONAL

  IF + SIMPLE PAST, WOULD /COULD/MIGHT/SHOULD

   If I had a dictionary , I would look this word up.

                                    


---

 Língua Inglesa – Prof . Adolfo Sá


IF-CLAUSES – SECOND CONDITIONAL

• Não há “WAS” no Second Conditional

         If I were you , I wouldn’t be so rude.


*** He talks as he WERE the boss.

                                     


---

Língua Inglesa – Prof . Adolfo Sá


IF-CLAUSES – THIRD CONDITIONAL

      IF + PAST PERFECT, WOULD HAVE + PAST PART.

 If I had left home earlier, you wouldn’t have been
                          late.

                                    


---

Língua Inglesa – Prof . Adolfo Sá


 IF-CLAUSES – ZERO CONDITIONAL

             IF + SIMPLE PRESENT, SIMPLE PRESENT

          If I press the button, the machine works.

IF-CLAUSES – MIXED CONDITIONALS
• As condicionais mistas podem discutir uma situação passada com um
  resultado hipotético no presente.

                             IF + PAST PERFECT, WOULD

If we had warmed up before the match, it would be much easier to play
                             right now.

              IF + SIMPLE PRESENT, SIMPLE PRESENT

            IF + SIMPLE PAST, WOULD HAVE + PAST PARTICIPLE

  Salli wouldn't be this fit now if she hadn't trained so much last year.

                    ORAÇÕES RELATIVAS

As orações relativas (relative clauses) em Inglês são divididas
   em non-defining (explicativas) e defining ou restrictive
                          (restritivas).

                                     


---

PRONOMES
               PRONOMES RELATIVOS
•   O aluno estuda sempre.


•   O aluno passa.

                      


---

PRONOMES
            PRONOMES RELATIVOS
•   O aluno estuda sempre.


•   O aluno passa.


           O aluno que estuda passa.

                     


---

PRONOMES
               PRONOMES RELATIVOS
•   My father bought a new car.


•   A new car was expensive.

                      


---

PRONOMES
            PRONOMES RELATIVOS
•   My father bought a new car.


•   A new car was expensive.


•   My father bought a new car that was expensive.
