QUANTIFIERS & DETERMINANTES
              TOO, TOO MUCH, TOO MANY, ENOUGH

• Sentido negativo → exagero
You’re driving too fast.
Você está dirigindo muito rápido.


He is too tired to concentrate.
Ele está muito cansado para se concentrar.


                       


---

QUANTIFIERS & DETERMINANTES
              TOO, TOO MUCH, TOO MANY, ENOUGH

• Sentido negativo → exagero
There is too much pepper in this food.
Tem pimenta demais nessa comida.


There were too many people on the boat.
Tinha gente demais no barco.


                       


---

QUANTIFIERS & DETERMINANTES
              TOO, TOO MUCH, TOO MANY, ENOUGH

•    Sentido negativo → exagero
It’s not too cold to go for a walk in the park.
– Não está frio demais/ Não está tão frio para se
fazer uma caminhada no parque.


How are you? Not too bad.
    – Como você está? Estou bem (não tão ruim assim).

QUANTIFIERS & DETERMINANTES
            All, Every e Each; All of e Each of
•    ALL = the group seen as one thing
•    EVERY = the group seen as a series of members
•    EACH = the members of the group seen individually

            He worked hard and saved all his money.

     Every person in the room give some money to pay the
                               beverages.

      He always brought a different present for each child.
