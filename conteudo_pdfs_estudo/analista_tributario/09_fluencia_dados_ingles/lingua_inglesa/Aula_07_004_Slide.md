QUESTION TAG
Palavras negativas (never, no, nobody, hardly,
scarcely, seldom, rarely e without) fazem com que
o question tag fique afirmativo.

  The bus never stops here, does it?
  You rarely say what you are thinking, do you?


                             INGLÊS – Adolfo Sá

QUESTION TAG
Frases afirmativas com o “AM” terão como
question tag “aren’t I”.

               I am sick, aren’t I?

---

QUESTION TAG
1. You know Paul’s new address, DON’T YOU?
2. Paul became a very good person, DIDN’T YOU?
3. Susan and I will have a good trip, WON’T WE?
4. Let’s buy a little butter, SHALL WE?
5. I have a doubt, DON’T I?
6. William never arrives home late, DOES HE?
7. The babies are crying, AREN’T THEY?
8. Helen hasn’t declared her opinion yet, HAS SHE?
9. Drive slowly, WILL YOU?
10. Let them know, WILL YOU?
11. I am not right, AM I?
12. Don’t talk too loud, WILL YOU?
13. I am late, AREN’T I?
                                         INGLÊS – Adolfo Sá

QUESTION TAG
As respostas curtas são aquelas que são dadas em resposta às
questions tags, geralmente yes (sim) ou no (não). Elas também são
dadas às perguntas curtas.


She can’t play the piano, can she? – No, she can’t.
Ela não sabe tocar piano, ela sabe? – Não, ela não sabe.


Has Mark come? – Yes, he has. / No, he hasn’t.
O Marcos veio? – Sim, ele veio. / Não, ele não veio.
