                  What do police officers do? By Emma Woolley
         The primary responsibility of police officers is to protect the public, or if
commissioned, the person, group or organization to which they are assigned.
Through detecting and preventing crime, police officers strive to maintain law
and order in their respective jurisdictions.
     Most new police officers work in general duty in patrol divisions, which
provides a broad range of experiences and assignments. General duty policing
involves patrolling assigned areas to enforce laws, protect public safety, and

         The primary responsibility of police officers is to protect the public, or if
